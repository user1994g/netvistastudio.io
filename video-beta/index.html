PUBLIC BETA

Not production-ready.

Features can be incomplete, controls can change, and bugs can happen. Test with copies of your media and keep backups of projects you care about.

PLATFORM DIFFERENCES

Not every tool is everywhere.

The macOS beta has the most complete 3D and animation tools. Windows and Linux preserve scene data, but do not have all the same editing features yet.

IN DEVELOPMENT

A little patience helps.

Complex effects, large scenes and high-resolution exports depend on your hardware. A resolution option is not a guarantee that every computer can render it.
