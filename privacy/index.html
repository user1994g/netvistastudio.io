01 / Who to contact

NetVista Studio is responsible for deciding how the personal information described in this notice is used to provide its websites and account services. For privacy questions and requests, contact jackmiller@netvistastudio.com.

This notice explains NetVista’s practices, not every independent website you may visit through a link. The separate News advertising notice describes the advertising setup prepared for our News hub.

02 / What we collect

Your account

When you create or use an account, we process your email address, account identifier, optional display name, password authentication information, and session and sign-in records. Passwords go to our authentication provider, Supabase, over HTTPS. The page does not put your password in a URL or save the password in browser storage.

Your email and a password are needed for password-based account access. Your display name is optional. Without an account you can browse public pages; the website’s download page and Watch catalogue require sign-in. App release files on GitHub remain publicly downloadable.

Watch progress and My List

Watch saves a compact record for each film you interact with: your account ID, the film ID, playback position, duration, whether you finished it, whether it is in My List, and last-watched and update times. This lets you resume on another signed-in device. That progress table does not contain your raw video, an event-by-event playback log, IP address or device location. Hosting and authentication providers may separately process connection and security logs.

Applications and messages

If you use the creative Applications form, we receive your name, email, area of work, optional portfolio link and message through Formspree. If you email us, we receive your email address and the information in your message. Please avoid including sensitive information or private project files unless needed for a specific enquiry.

Page delivery and optional analytics

Visiting a website sends connection information such as your IP address, browser details, request time and requested resource to the services delivering it. If you allow optional analytics on an eligible page, Microsoft Clarity can also receive page interactions, device/browser information, identifiers and session-recording information. See browser storage and analytics for your choices.

04 / Where things are saved

On the account service

Supabase hosts authentication and the saved Watch progress database. Watch updates your progress while playing and at events such as pause and closing the player. A final page-close save is best effort: a connection failure or immediately closing the browser can mean the latest few seconds are not saved.

Your progress is associated with your signed-in account, not a public profile. My List and Continue Watching are separate choices. Unticking a film in Continue Watching hides it from that row but keeps its resume point and My List choice. Starting a deliberate new playback session can add it to Continue Watching again.

On your browser or device

The browser stores authentication tokens so the account can stay signed in. The main site and Watch use separate browser-storage areas because they are different web origins, even though the same account works on both. Signing out on one browser is different from deleting the account.

Signing in to this website does not itself upload your local editing projects, photos, video source files, models or exports. Files you save in the native apps stay where you save them unless you deliberately use a sharing or external-service feature. Those features, downloads, optional model downloads and operating-system storage can involve separate services and permissions.

07 / How long it stays

Account details and saved Watch records are retained to provide your account and requested saved-viewing features until you delete them, delete the account, or we have another reason to remove them. There is currently no automatic age-based expiry for a Watch progress row.

For support messages and applications, the retention decision depends on handling your enquiry, any ongoing collaboration, and a need to resolve a dispute or meet a legal requirement. Request a review or deletion if information is no longer needed. Relevant security records may be retained to investigate misuse or meet legal obligations, rather than being used as viewing-history records.

Copies may remain for a limited period in providers’ backups or operational/security logs according to the relevant service’s configuration and retention schedule. Deleting a live account is not a promise that every backup copy disappears immediately. Backup copies should not be used for ordinary account operation after deletion; if a restoration is needed, the deletion must be reapplied. Contact us if you need details about the records or retention arrangements relevant to your request.

Browser storage follows the controls described above. Local editing project files, exports and downloads are not deleted from your device by deleting a website account.

09 / Deleting your account

Open Your account, sign in, and use Delete account. Review the warning and complete the confirmation. Deletion affects the shared NetVista account used by Editor and Watch, not only the site where you started.

After a successful deletion, the authentication account and its associated saved Watch records are removed from the live service. You lose account access, My List and resume positions. The deletion page must confirm success; an error message means you should not assume the request finished. You can contact us if the control is unavailable or you need help with a privacy request.

Signing out is not deletion. Unticking Continue Watching is not deletion of the resume record. Deleting the account does not erase files already downloaded to a device, your local editing projects, an email conversation, or all independent records a provider may lawfully retain. For those separate records, use the relevant controls or contact us. Backup and legal-retention qualifications are described above.
