02 BEHIND THE CONNECTION

50G PON.
Thinking bigger.

THE TECHNOLOGY IS REAL. OUR NETWORK IS NOT.

The idea imagines a full-fibre access network using 50G PON — a 50-Gigabit-capable passive optical network. A shared optical line would connect an imagined exchange to homes through passive splitters.

That is the design behind our fictional 500 Mbps, 1 Gig, 2 Gig and 20 Gig plans. NetVista does not own or operate this infrastructure.

50G is a shared network line rate. It is not a promise of 50 Gbps dedicated to every home. Plan speeds, network capacity and a device’s achievable throughput are different things.

Not a deployment announcement. The symmetrical plan figures are design targets only. No equipment, capacity, coverage or delivery has been validated for NetVista.

Real technology reference: ITU-T G.9804.3 · 50G-PON specification ↗. This reference is not an endorsement of NetVista.

05 JUST SO WE’RE CLEAR

A few
real answers.

Is NetVista Fibre a real internet provider?+

No. This is a fictional ISP design concept created for fun by NetVista Studio. We do not operate an internet service or a broadband network.

Can I buy a connection or check my postcode?+

No. There are no plans for sale, orders, payments or real coverage checks here. We don’t collect postcodes or addresses on this page.

Are the plans and monthly prices real?+

No. The 500 Mbps, 1 Gig, 2 Gig and 20 Gig plans, prices and features are fictional examples. “Preview this plan” only updates the on-page calculator. It does not place an order, reserve a connection or create a contract.

Does NetVista actually use 50G PON?+

No. 50G PON is a real optical access technology, but this page only imagines using it. NetVista does not operate a 50G PON network. Its shared line rate is not a dedicated per-home speed guarantee.

Is there a launch date?+

No launch date is announced. This page is an idea, not a promise to build or supply a network.

Does the playground save anything about me?+

The calculator runs in your browser and keeps its selections only while the page is open. This concept has no sign-in, tracking scripts, advertising, payment system or forms. Normal hosting security and request logs may still apply. Read the NetVista privacy notice for information about our wider website.
